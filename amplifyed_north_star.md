# AmplifyEd — North Star Document
## Master Roadmap + Core Tenets (2025 Edition)

## Core Tenets (Guiding Principles)
1. Safety
- Psychological safety first
- Data minimization
- Anonymous-first design
- No teacher surveillance
- No admin misuse
- No identifiable metadata

2. Teacher Voice
- Teacher empowerment
- Bottom-up PD design
- Listening > lecturing
- Responsive learning

3. Simplicity & Speed
- One-tap interactions
- Nothing extra on the screen
- Fast onboarding
- No account creation for participants

4. Respect for Time
- Sessions under 60 minutes
- Quick signal reading
- No heavy configuration

5. Real-Time Intelligence
- Live emotional awareness
- Insight surfaces not dashboards
- Actionable signals

6. Facilitator Support, Not Replacement
- AI augments, never replaces
- Trainer stays in control
- AI stays backstage unless invited

7. Radical Transparency
- Clear “what is being tracked” messaging
- Permanent no-surveillance guarantee
- Full control for facilitator

8. Universal Accessibility
- Cross-platform
- Works on old devices
- Low bandwidth
- Screen-reader friendly

9. Elegant UX
- Minimalism
- Dark-mode first
- Low cognitive load

10. Research-Backed PD
- Adult learning principles
- Psychological safety
- Improv-inspired facilitation
- Feedback-loop-driven learning
