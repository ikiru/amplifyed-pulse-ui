# PHASE 5 — GOVERNANCE

## Human Signal Stewardship

---

## Purpose

Phase 5 establishes governance for how human signals are **held across time** inside AmplifyEd *after* safety, voice, and structure are complete.

This phase does not introduce new systems, features, or intelligence.
It defines **limits**, not capabilities.

Phase 5 exists to prevent memory from becoming control.

---

## Governing Principle

**Continuity without possession**

Human signals may inform future understanding, but they must never:

* define identity
* assert truth
* predict behavior
* outlive their context

The system may support remembrance.
It may never claim interpretation.

---

## Scope

Phase 5 governs:

* how signals age
* how meaning persists or dissolves
* how prior context may be referenced
* how trainers retain narrative authority
* how participants are protected from historical lock-in

Phase 5 does **not** govern:

* safety enforcement
* moderation
* scoring
* evaluation
* optimization
* automation
* prediction

---

## Core Rules

### 1. Temporal Humility

All signals are time-bound expressions.
Authority decays with time, regardless of signal intensity or frequency.

Persistence does not imply relevance.

---

### 2. Context, Not Artifact

Only contextual understanding may carry forward.

Raw signals, measurements, or expressions must not be reused as evidence or proof in later moments.

---

### 3. Human Narrative Authority

Only humans contextualize humans.

Trainers may reference prior context, but the system must never:

* draw conclusions
* suggest causality
* frame past signals as explanation for present behavior

---

### 4. Right to Dissolve

Forgetting is a valid and protected outcome.

The absence of retained signal is not an error condition.
In some cases, it is the correct and safest result.

---

### 5. Non-Convergent Meaning

Multiple interpretations of the same moment may coexist.

Phase 5 explicitly rejects:

* forced synthesis
* averaging of meaning
* resolution pressure

Ambiguity is allowed to stand.

---

## Explicit Prohibitions

Under Phase 5 governance, the system must not:

* assign identity labels across time
* re-score or re-weight past signals
* compare current behavior against historical baselines
* reuse prior context invisibly
* frame insights as optimization outcomes
* convert narrative into metrics

If a behavior resembles prediction, profiling, or classification, it is out of scope.

---

## Trainer Safeguards

* Trainers retain full interpretive authority.
* Any reference to prior context must be visible, interruptible, and dismissible.
* No historical signal may override live judgment.

The system supports sensemaking.
It never replaces it.

---

## Participant Safeguards

* A participant’s past expression must not define future treatment.
* No individual may be reduced to their most extreme moment.
* Growth must remain narratively open.

The system must allow people to change without being archived into themselves.

---

## Phase 5 Completion Criteria

Phase 5 is considered complete when:

* Memory cannot be mistaken for truth
* History cannot silently assert authority
* Ambiguity is preserved without pressure
* No automation gravity exists

At that point, Phase 5 governance is **locked**.

---

## Closing Statement

Phase 5 ensures AmplifyEd remains a place where:

* signals are honored
* meaning is contextual
* memory is careful
* and people remain larger than their data

This phase governs restraint.
