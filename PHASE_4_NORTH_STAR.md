# Phase 4 — North Star

**Assisted Awareness (Trainer-Gated)**

---

## Status

**Approved and locked.**
Phase 4 intent is defined here and must not be reinterpreted.

---

## Phase 4 Purpose

Phase 4 exists to help trainers **notice patterns they may otherwise miss**,
**without removing agency, judgment, or responsibility**.

Phase 4 does **not** act.
Phase 4 does **not** decide.
Phase 4 does **not** intervene.

It supports *human awareness*, not system authority.

---

## Core North Star Statement (Authoritative)

> **Phase 4 introduces contextual awareness without behavioral control.**
>
> The system may suggest *where to look*,
> but never *what to do*.

If this statement stops being true, Phase 4 has failed.

---

## What Phase 4 Is Allowed to Do

Phase 4 **may**:

* Detect **patterns across multiple moments**
* Surface **contextual observations** (not conclusions)
* Offer **trainer-initiated prompts** such as:

  * “You may want to review…”
  * “A pattern is emerging across…”
* Allow trainers to **opt in** to awareness views
* Frame system output as *possibility*, never certainty
* Be slow, conservative, and interruptible
* Consume Phase 3 outputs as **immutable inputs**

**All Phase 4 surfacing must be explicitly user-requested and must never appear automatically.**

Phase 4 operates on **trainer consent**, not system urgency.

---

## What Phase 4 Must Never Do

Phase 4 must **never**:

* Recommend actions
* Rank urgency or priority
* Trigger alerts automatically
* Interrupt a live session
* Override trainer judgment
* Act without explicit trainer intent
* Learn or adapt silently
* Create pressure, guilt, or evaluation signals
* **Auto-surface insights or patterns without trainer request**

If a feature feels like:

* guidance
* judgment
* performance assessment
* automation

…it does **not belong in Phase 4**.

---

## Trainer Mental Model (Locked)

> “The system helps me *notice*.
> I still decide *meaning* and *response*.”

If a trainer can say:

> “The system told me to…”

Phase 4 has crossed a line.

---

## Psychological Safety Constraint (Hard Guardrail)

Phase 4 must always be:

* **Non-urgent**
* **Non-evaluative**
* **Non-performative**
* **Non-persistent by default**

Awareness fades unless the trainer chooses to hold it.
Nothing follows the trainer unless invited.

---

## Relationship to Phase 3

Phase 4 **builds on Phase 3** but does not modify it.

* Phase 3 outputs are **immutable inputs**
* Phase 4 never alters interpretation logic
* Phase 4 consumes, never rewrites
* Phase 3 remains observational
* Phase 4 adds *contextual framing only*

If Phase 3 feels calm,
Phase 4 must feel **calm + optional**.

---

## Phase Boundary Warning

⚠️ Any feature that includes:

* recommendations
* prioritization
* intervention logic
* adaptive behavior
* system confidence escalation

**does not belong in Phase 4**
and must be deferred to a later phase with new safeguards.

---

## Success Criteria

Phase 4 is successful if:

* Trainers feel *more aware*, not more judged
* Nothing feels automated
* The system never interrupts
* Trainers can ignore Phase 4 completely with no penalty
* The platform remains psychologically safe under stress

---

## Failure Modes (Explicit)

Phase 4 has failed if:

* Trainers feel watched
* Trainers feel evaluated
* Trainers feel pressured to act
* Silence feels like failure
* The system feels smarter than the human

---

## One-Sentence Memory Anchor

> **Phase 4 helps trainers notice patterns — never actions.**

---

*End of Phase 4 North Star*
