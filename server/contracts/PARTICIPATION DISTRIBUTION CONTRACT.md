# PARTICIPATION DISTRIBUTION CONTRACT
**AmplifyEd TrainerView — Tier-1 Signal**

---

## 1. Purpose

Participation Distribution surfaces **how widely ideas are being carried in the room**, so a trainer can decide **where to invite more voices**.

It does not judge fairness, quality, or intent.
It reveals **structure**, not behavior.

---

## 2. Scope & Principles

### 2.1 What Participation Measures
Participation Distribution measures the **breadth of distinct participation per thread**.

It reflects:
- how many unique contributors engage with an idea
- how widely a thread is picked up

It does not reflect:
- frequency of speaking
- message quality
- correctness
- dominance or authority

---

### 2.2 What Participation Is Not
Participation Distribution is not:
- an equity score
- a dominance detector
- a behavioral evaluation
- a recommendation engine

False positives and ambiguity are acceptable.

---

## 3. Scored Unit

- **Unit of scoring:** Thread / concept
- **Never scored:** Individuals

Each thread maintains an internal, ephemeral measure of participation breadth.

---

## 4. Signal Inputs (Breadth Growth)

Participation breadth increases when:
- a new, distinct participant contributes to a thread
- contributions occur within the current focus window

Each participant is counted **once per thread**.

---

## 5. Decay & Reset

### 5.1 Passive Decay
Participation breadth decays gradually over time to reflect **current conversation shape**, not history.

Decay rate matches Tier-1 standards.

---

### 5.2 Focus Change Reset
On focus change:
- all participation footprints reset

Participation does not persist across focuses.

---

## 6. Visibility Rules

### 6.1 Trainer Visibility
Trainers can see:
- participation footprints per thread
- relative breadth across threads

Trainers cannot see:
- contributor identities
- counts or percentages
- rankings or labels

---

### 6.2 Audience Visibility
Audience members cannot see:
- participation footprints
- aggregate participation patterns

Participation is observational, trainer-only.

---

## 7. TrainerView Placement (LOCKED)

Participation Distribution appears:
- at the **thread header level** as a footprint bar
- optionally in the **left column** as a summary list

It never appears on individual messages.

---

## 8. Visual Language

### 8.1 Thread Footprint

Thread: Exit tickets vs formative checks
[ footprint ▓▓▓░░░░ ]


- Fixed width
- Filled blocks indicate distinct participation
- No numbers
- No labels
- No color escalation

---

## 9. Design Constraints

Participation Distribution must:
- avoid identifying individuals
- avoid moral or equity framing
- tolerate silence
- decay naturally
- function without AI

AI is not required and not authoritative.

---

## 10. Governance Notes

- Participation reveals structure, not behavior
- Trainers decide whether to act
- No automated intervention is implied

---

## 11. Non-Goals

This system will not:
- rank participants
- assess fairness
- enforce balance
- persist participation history

---

## 12. Final Lock Statement

> Participation Distribution exists to show how widely ideas are being carried in the room. It observes structure, decays naturally, and never evaluates people.
