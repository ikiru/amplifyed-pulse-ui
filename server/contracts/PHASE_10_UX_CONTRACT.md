# 📘 AmplifyEd — Phase 10 UX Decisions (LOCKED)

**Phase:** 10
**Theme:** Interpretation, Use & Organizational Boundaries (Without Extraction)
**Scope:** UX posture, language, and governance meaning only
**Status:** COMPLETE

Phase 10 defines how AmplifyEd outputs are **spoken about, interpreted, and acted upon by humans** after a session ends — without allowing insight to become leverage, surveillance, or authority.

Nothing in this phase introduces new data, artifacts, or outputs.
Phase 8 (Lifecycle) and Phase 9 (Memory) are closed and binding.

---

## Core Principle

> **Insight must never become leverage.**

The value of AmplifyEd is lost the moment its outputs are used to justify power, attribution, or accountability.

---

## Phase 10.1 — Post-Session Trainer Language Guardrails

### Allowed Trainer Posture

After a session ends, trainers may speak only in ways that are:

* reflective
* personal
* inward-facing
* uncertain

Examples of acceptable posture:

* “I’m reflecting on how I framed that section.”
* “There were moments I want to approach differently next time.”
* “I noticed places where I felt unsure how things were landing.”

**The trainer is always the subject of the sentence.**

---

### Prohibited Trainer Language

Trainers must not:

* summarize the room
* interpret audience intent
* narrate group sentiment
* explain outcomes
* assign causality

Explicitly disallowed language includes:

* “The group felt…”
* “People were resistant to…”
* “They didn’t engage because…”
* “It was clear that…”

Even if anonymized.
Even if aggregated.
Even if accurate.

---

### Attribution Boundary

> **After a session ends, there is no ‘they.’**

The audience cannot be referenced as a coherent actor once the room dissolves.

---

## Phase 10.2 — Organizational Interpretation Limits

### What Organizations May Not Infer

Organizations must not infer:

* individual agreement or dissent
* engagement or buy-in
* emotional state
* readiness for change
* compliance or resistance
* consensus or majority opinion

Presence does not grant interpretive authority.

---

### What Organizations May Not Request

Organizations must not request:

* attendance proof
* participation metrics
* engagement scores
* message summaries
* representative quotes
* cross-session comparisons
* trend lines across teams

The use of AmplifyEd does not entitle leadership to insight extraction.

---

### What Organizations May Not Do

Organizations must not:

* reference sessions in evaluations
* justify decisions using session artifacts
* mandate follow-up based on session output
* treat silence as dissent
* treat engagement as consent

---

### Narrow Allowance

Organizations may:

* reflect on facilitation quality
* examine their own uncertainty
* adjust how they listen
* change how questions are framed

They may not change beliefs about people.

---

## Phase 10.3 — Non-Transferability & Context Collapse

### Non-Transferability Principle

> **AmplifyEd insights are context-bound and non-transferable.**

Insights must not be:

* reused across sessions
* compared over time
* cited in leadership discussions
* embedded in planning materials
* framed as representative sentiment

Even if anonymized.
Even if aggregated.

---

### Context Collapse Risk

> **The moment an insight leaves the room, it begins answering questions it was never meant to answer.**

AmplifyEd is designed to prevent this collapse, not optimize it.

---

### Boundary on Learning

Learning may occur only as:

* facilitator growth
* improved listening posture
* reframed questions
* changed facilitation behavior

Learning may not appear as conclusions about people.

---

## Phase 10 — Global Invariants

Across all post-session interpretation and use:

* Insight does not imply intent
* Silence does not imply meaning
* Presence does not imply consent
* Memory does not imply authority
* Safety overrides usefulness
* Meaning does not travel
