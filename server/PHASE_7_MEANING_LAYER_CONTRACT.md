# PHASE 7 — MEANING LAYER CONTRACT

**Status:** FINAL / LOCKED  
**Applies To:** Phase 7 and all future phases (8+)  
**Last Updated:** Phase 7.5 Completion

---

## 1. Purpose of the Meaning Layer

The Meaning Layer exists to:

- Describe **patterns in interaction structure**
- Preserve **ambiguity and uncertainty**
- Support **reflection without judgment**
- Prevent interpretation from becoming control

The Meaning Layer does **not** exist to:

- Evaluate people
- Judge effectiveness
- Predict outcomes
- Drive behavior
- Trigger actions

AmplifyEd is a **listening system**, not a judging system.

---

## 2. Inputs — What Meaning May Read

The Meaning Layer may read **only aggregated, structural signals**, including:

- Interaction frequency over time
- Reply chain structure (depth, branching)
- Timing intervals between interactions
- Directional flow of interaction (group-level only)
- Presence or absence of interaction signals

### Explicit Exclusions

The Meaning Layer may **not** read:

- Message content
- User identity
- Emotional signals
- Pulse, safety, or focus states
- Any single individual’s behavior

---

## 3. Allowed Meaning Categories (Final)

The Meaning Layer may produce **only** the following categories.

### Tier 2 — Safest to Surface

1. **Thread Depth Emergence**  
   Structural extension of reply chains over time.  
   Content-agnostic and non-evaluative.

2. **Silence Density**  
   Periods with fewer interaction intents, treated as neutral.  
   Silence is never framed as absence, deficit, or resistance.

---

### Tier 1 — Surfaced Only With Guardrails

3. **Participation Distribution**  
   The shape and spread of interaction across the group.  
   Never ranked, never tied to individuals.

4. **Response Latency Drift**  
   Comparative timing changes relative to earlier reference windows.  
   No absolute speed labels permitted.

5. **Interaction Trend Direction**  
   Increasing, decreasing, or stable interaction frequency over time.  
   Directional only, never evaluative.

---

### Tier 0 — Internal Only (Never Surfaced)

6. **Interaction Asymmetry**  
   Directional imbalance of interaction flows.  
   Permanently internal due to misinterpretation risk.

---

## 4. Forbidden Meanings (Permanent)

The Meaning Layer will **never** infer, store, or surface:

- Individual engagement
- Understanding or confusion
- Emotional state
- Motivation or intent
- Agreement or disagreement
- Trainer effectiveness
- Session success or failure
- Causality
- Prediction
- Optimal thresholds
- Identity-based traits

These meanings are **structurally disallowed**, not deferred.

---

## 5. Language Rules (Binding)

All meaning statements must:

- Be **descriptive**, not evaluative
- Use **comparative**, not absolute language
- Preserve **uncertainty explicitly**
- Remain **group-level only**

### Allowed Language Types

- Directional: increasing, decreasing, stable
- Structural: distribution, density, chain length
- Temporal: earlier, later, relative to a reference window

### Forbidden Language Types

- Evaluative (good, bad, effective, weak)
- Psychological (engaged, bored, confused, motivated)
- Causal (because, led to, resulted in)
- Normative (should, must, optimal)
- Predictive (will, likely to, expected to)

If language implies judgment when taken out of context, it is invalid.

---

## 6. Visibility Rules (Binding)

Meaning visibility is governed by tier:

- **Tier 0:** Never surfaced
- **Tier 1:** Surfaced only with framing and guardrails
- **Tier 2:** Surfaced cautiously and optionally

### Universal Prohibitions

Surfaced meaning may **never** include:

- Individual identifiers
- Rankings or scores
- Benchmarks or targets
- Alerts or calls to action
- Automated recommendations

Meaning informs; it never directs.

---

## 7. Reversibility & Non-Coupling Guarantees

The Meaning Layer must be:

- Fully removable without corrupting data
- Independent of transport, UI, and pipelines
- Downstream-only (no upstream control)
- Optional by design

Deleting the Meaning Layer must not alter:

- Messages
- Interaction intents
- Session state
- Participant experience

---

## 8. Enforcement Rule

Any future feature, phase, or proposal must answer:

> “Where does this fit within the Meaning Layer Contract?”

- If it extends meaning, it must comply with this contract
- If it violates a prohibition, it does not ship
- If it bypasses meaning, it is rejected

---

## 9. Final Assertion

The Meaning Layer exists to:

> **Make patterns visible without making people visible.**

This contract is the ethical boundary of AmplifyEd and is binding on all future development.

# PHASE 5 — GOVERNANCE ADDENDUM  
**Binding Forward Constraint for Phases 6+**

**Status:** FINAL / APPEND-ONLY  
**Effective As Of:** Completion of Phase 7  
**Applies To:** All subsequent phases (6–9+)

---

## Purpose of This Addendum

This addendum formally binds the principles and prohibitions of **Phase 5 — Governance** to all systems introduced in later phases, including:

- Message transport and structure (Phase 6)
- Interaction intent (Phase 6.2–6.3)
- Meaning and interpretation layers (Phase 7)
- Any future UX, persistence, or reporting layers (Phase 8+)

This document introduces **no new behavior**.  
It exists solely to prevent drift by making implicit constraints explicit.

---

## 1. Authority Inheritance Rule

All systems introduced after Phase 5 **inherit Phase 5 governance constraints by default**, including but not limited to:

- Interpretation layers
- Aggregation logic
- Meaning categories
- Visualization and surfacing mechanisms
- Persistence and replay systems

No later phase may claim exemption from Phase 5 governance principles.

---

## 2. Meaning Does Not Confer Authority

The presence of **interaction intent**, **structural patterns**, or **meaning categories** does **not** grant authority to:

- Evaluate individuals
- Judge participation
- Infer understanding
- Predict outcomes
- Trigger actions
- Recommend responses

Meaning may exist.  
Authority does not emerge from meaning.

This distinction is permanent.

---

## 3. Prohibition on Automated Judgment

No system within AmplifyEd may:

- Translate meaning into action
- Escalate interpretation automatically
- Suggest corrective behavior
- Frame outcomes as success or failure
- Optimize toward participation targets

All judgment remains human, contextual, and optional.

Automation may support visibility.  
Automation may never replace discretion.

---

## 4. Cross-Layer Non-Coupling Guarantee

The following couplings are explicitly forbidden:

- Meaning → Trainer action
- Signal → Evaluation
- Pattern → Recommendation
- Aggregation → Obligation
- Silence → Deficit

Layers may observe downstream signals.  
No layer may exert upstream control.

---

## 5. Human Agency Preservation

The system must always preserve:

- The facilitator’s right to ignore surfaced information
- The participant’s right to remain unread
- The organization’s inability to extract hidden evaluation

No surfaced signal may imply a required response.

Silence, ambiguity, and non-response are valid states.

---

## 6. Memory Is Not Truth

Any persistence of interaction, intent, or meaning:

- Is historical, not authoritative
- May not be treated as ground truth
- May not be replayed as judgment
- May not be used for performance assessment

Memory supports reflection.  
Memory does not define reality.

---

## 7. Enforcement Clause

Any future feature, phase, or proposal must answer:

> “Does this violate Phase 5 governance, even indirectly?”

If the answer is unclear, the feature does not ship.

If the answer is “only in certain contexts,” the feature does not ship.

Governance ambiguity defaults to rejection.

---

## 8. Final Assertion

Phase 5 establishes that AmplifyEd:

- Observes without surveillance
- Remembers without judgment
- Surfaces without directing
- Listens without deciding

This addendum ensures those principles remain intact as capability grows.
