# Phase 4 — Governance  
**Assisted Awareness (Trainer-Gated)**

---

## Status

**Approved, authorized, and locked.**

This document is the **single source of truth** for Phase 4.  
All other Phase 4 documents are superseded by this file.

---

## Purpose

Phase 4 exists to help trainers **notice patterns they may otherwise miss**,  
**without removing agency, judgment, or responsibility**.

Phase 4 does **not** act.  
Phase 4 does **not** decide.  
Phase 4 does **not** intervene.

It supports *human awareness*, not system authority.

---

## Core Principle (Non-Negotiable)

> **Phase 4 introduces contextual awareness without behavioral control.**
>
> The system may suggest *where to look*,  
> but never *what to do*.

If this statement stops being true, Phase 4 has failed.

---

## Trainer Mental Model (Locked)

> “The system helps me *notice*.  
> I still decide *meaning* and *response*.”

If a trainer can say:

> “The system told me to…”

Phase 4 has crossed a line.

---

## What Phase 4 Is Allowed to Do

Phase 4 **may**:

- Detect **patterns across multiple moments**
- Surface **contextual observations** (not conclusions)
- Provide **trainer-initiated, pull-only awareness**
- Frame output as *possibility*, never certainty
- Be slow, calm, conservative, and interruptible
- Consume Phase 3 outputs as **immutable inputs**

**Nothing in Phase 4 may surface automatically.**  
All awareness is explicitly requested by the trainer.

---

## What Phase 4 Must Never Do

Phase 4 must **never**:

- Recommend actions
- Rank urgency or priority
- Trigger alerts or nudges
- Interrupt a live session
- Override trainer judgment
- Act without explicit trainer intent
- Learn, adapt, or evolve silently
- Create pressure, guilt, or evaluation signals
- Auto-surface insights of any kind

If something feels like guidance, judgment, automation, or evaluation,  
it does **not belong in Phase 4**.

---

## Psychological Safety Constraints

Phase 4 must always be:

- **Non-urgent**
- **Non-evaluative**
- **Non-performative**
- **Non-persistent by default**

Awareness fades unless the trainer chooses to keep it.  
Nothing follows the trainer unless invited.

---

## Relationship to Phase 3 (Hard Boundary)

- Phase 3 is **closed and immutable**
- Phase 4 consumes Phase 3 outputs only
- No reinterpretation, reweighting, or correction of Phase 3
- No feedback loop from Phase 4 back into Phase 3

If Phase 3 behavior is questioned, Phase 4 work stops.

---

## Authorization & Entry Conditions

Phase 4 work is authorized **only** under the following conditions:

- Phase 3 remains locked and unchanged
- Trainer-gated, pull-only behavior is preserved
- Phase 4 features are fully optional and ignorable
- Phase 4 can be disabled without breaking Phase 3
- Rollback is trivial and lossless

Phase 4 is authorized for **planning and design only** unless explicitly expanded.

---

## Stop Conditions (Immediate)

Phase 4 work must halt immediately if:

- Trainers feel watched or evaluated
- Awareness becomes persistent by default
- Silence feels like failure
- The system begins to feel directive
- Automation pressure emerges

Any one of these invalidates Phase 4.

---

## One-Sentence Anchor

> **Phase 4 helps trainers notice patterns — never actions.**
